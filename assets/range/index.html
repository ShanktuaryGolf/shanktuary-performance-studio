Range Game Modes
⛳ Free Practice
Active
Standard open driving range with full telemetry, custom target distance & fairway corridors. No game scoring rules.
🎯 Target Challenge
Active
10-shot / 75-point precision game. Hit concentric rings on the target green to score points and earn proximity awards.
🪜 Distance Ladder
Active
Starts at 20 yards. Hit the green to climb the ladder as the pin moves back by a random 10 to 20 yards on every successful shot!
📍 Closest to Pin
Active
3-shot shootout measuring closest distance to the pin in feet. Test your approach accuracy.
💥 Long Drive
Active
Max distance shootout. Only shots that finish within the fairway boundary corridor qualify.
