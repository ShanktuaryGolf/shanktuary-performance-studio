# 3D Asset Attributions

The Shanktuary WebGPU 3D Driving Range uses the following amazing 3D models licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY-4.0):

* **"Realistic Fir Trees Pack"** - Evergreen pines & firs with multi-tier LODs.
* **"Five Birch Trees Pack"** - Low-poly white bark birch trees with leaf atlas textures.
* **"Maple Trees Pack"** - Game-ready Acer / Maple saplings, medium, and large broadleaf trees.
* **"Hero TreeGen Foliage Tree"** - Dense broadleaf canopy tree with vine accents.
* **"Low Poly Trees Pack"** - Created by [LOLIPOP](https://sketchfab.com/lolipop)
* **"Stone Bridge"** - Created by [mfhscoobydoo](https://sketchfab.com/mfhscoobydoo)
* **"Golf Range Targets & Signs"** - Created by [Gravity Jack](https://sketchfab.com/gravityjack)
* **"Bushes and Grass Clumps"** - Created by [VictorSantos76](https://sketchfab.com/victorsantos76)
* **"Water Shader / Textures"** - Created by [Anthony Monteagudo](https://sketchfab.com/anthonymonteagudo)

Thank you to the talented creators on Sketchfab for providing these models to the community.
